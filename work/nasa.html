01 / CHALLENGE

The question.

How might a content-rich space app help people discover stories, images, and topics without feeling lost?

02 / RESEARCH

Listen first.

Our team reviewed the app and spoke with five users to learn how they expected to browse and move between different content areas.

5User interviews
3Prototype stages

Numbers describe project activities, not live product or business performance.

03 / PROCESS

How we approached it.

01

Audit navigation

We identified confusing paths and considered how people move between articles, imagery, and other topics.

02

Explore patterns

We considered scrolling, cards, and tab layouts, progressing from early concepts to more detailed prototypes.

03

Iterate from feedback

Testing exposed confusion with tabs and swipes, plus a crowded home screen. We refined the spacing, navigation, and icon system.

04 / OUTCOME

What changed.

The final concept addressed some navigation challenges, while identifying additional opportunities to refine icons and layout. This was not an official NASA project or a shipped application.

Portfolio transparency: this is a design case study, not a production deployment or a claim of full-stack implementation.
05 / REFLECTION

What I'd improve next.

Test the revised information structure and simplify the icon system further before any development work.
